今日要点

安全测试已证明：模型能力必须与控制面一起上线

BBC 报道称，Anthropic 在网络安全测试中发现其模型曾进入三家机构的计算机系统；这发生在 OpenAI 报告类似事件之后。这里最重要的不是把受控测试等同于现实攻击，而是能力评估已不能脱离权限、隔离环境、日志与事故披露。对部署方而言，是否可以撤销访问、复核工具调用和限制横向移动，正成为和模型质量同级的上线条件。

AI 资本开支的问号落在利用率与回报周期

《纽约时报》报道，大型科技公司继续提高 AI 相关支出，亚马逊的资本开支同比大幅增长，市场也在重新审视投入节奏。模型竞争因此不只是参数与基准竞争，还取决于数据中心能否持续被高价值工作负载填满。读财报时，应把云收入、推理需求和折旧压力分开观察，而非把“投入增加”直接读作增长结论。

AI 与科技

Gemini Robotics 2 把控制范围扩展到全身动作

Google DeepMind 表示，新版 Gemini Robotics 模型可支持人形机器人的全身动作控制，而前一版本主要面向上肢。能力范围扩大并不等于可靠性已被证明：真实机器人还要面对接触力、环境变化、失效保护和人机共处等问题。值得追踪的是在多样任务上的成功率、恢复策略和安全约束，而非单次演示。

安全智能体的难点是部署边界，不是多调用一次模型

NVIDIA 的部署建议把数字同事式智能体与权限、数据访问和工具使用并列讨论。对于企业实践，最小权限、分段凭证、输出验证与审计日志应当构成默认架构，而不是事故后的补丁。智能体越能执行外部动作，越应把“能做什么”落实为可测试的策略。

Apple 将 UMAP 的内部近邻图视为可分析对象

Apple 研究团队提出，不应只看 UMAP 降维后的二维投影，也可直接分析其构建的 kNN 图。文中以 PageRank、k-core 等图算法识别代表点与稠密结构，试图减少二维投影带来的解释偏差。对数据分析者而言，这是把可视化从“结论图”拉回原始邻接结构的一种方法。

@michaelxpettis · 请求账号 @michaelxpettis · 2026-07-30 09:20:36 UTC · 原创I just finished re-reading Paul Volcker and Toyoo Gyohten's book, published in 1992, on the management of the global financial system during three previous turbulent decades. This account, by two of its main actors, may be a bit specialized for most readers, but financial history junkies like me will greatly enjoy it. There were for me at least three points especially relevant today that came out of this re-reading. First, both authors made very clear that external economic policies are always subordinated to domestic economic and political needs. This makes real international coordination of financial, monetary and economic policies impossible because there is almost no way to prevent domestic imbalances from spilling over into and distorting the global economy. Volcker seems to think (although Gyohten came across as a little more skeptical) that the best way to address this problem is for major economies to find better ways to strengthen international coordination. I would argue, however, that we've never found a way to do this successfully, and that perhaps a more realistic response might be to restructure global trade and capital regimes in ways that more formally limit spillovers, perhaps along the lines Keynes proposed at Bretton Woods. This might include limiting unfettered financial flows. The second interesting point is that the different concerns expressed by Volcker and Gyohten showed how much difference there is between a finance-oriented view of the economy and a production-oriented view. Volcker, for example, seemed always to worry about conditions that might lead to excessive depreciation of the dollar, whereas Gyohten seemed to worry far more about conditions that might lead to excessive appreciation of the yen. Volcker cared more, it seems, about the stability of the US financial system while Gyohten cared more about the strength of the Japanese manufacturing sector. Finally, and this has almost become a cliche, Volcker was very American in that he tended to see the global economy mainly as an expression of US behavior and actions, both for good and bad, whereas Gyohten tended more than Volcker to place events in the context not just of American policies and conditions, but also of Germany, British, French, Japanese, and global conditions and policies. As a rule I always recommend reading "out of date" books by very wise practitioners because it is useful to see just how wrong we often are about future events. For example both Volcker and Gyohten thought that USD/JPY coordination would be among the most important issues in the first decades of the 21st Century, and they were both interested in the implications of a Japanese economy overtaking that of the US. It is hard to convince millennials that these concerns weren't as dumb then as they might seem today.↗

I heard multiple predictions that RSI would be achieved next year (one could argue the labs have already achieved partial RSI – see METR’s recent report on the pace of Anthropic LLM advances), as well as that LLMs capable of fully generalizing across robotics tasks/environments would arrive in 2027.

With general-purpose robots/humanoids, it looks like hardware engineering might soon be the biggest remaining obstacle to mass deployment. I’m not currently baking a robotics inflection into my expectations for semis, and stuff like this usually doesn’t transpire overnight, but if/when it does happen, there are obviously big implications for memory, compute, power semis, sensors, etc.

But they also provided reasons to be optimistic about what happens over longer timeframes.

For now, a relatively small number of early adopters/power users (at the corporate, department/team and individual levels) are driving a huge % of non-coding token growth.
